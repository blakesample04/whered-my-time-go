## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Where'd my time go? isn't another screen-time tracker that just guilts you about your numbers. It's built around one idea: the time you take back should go *somewhere* — toward something you actually care about.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set a daily target for your discretionary screen time.
• Log your usage (manually or import a CSV export from your phone's built-in screen-time report).
• Every minute under your target gets banked as reclaimed time — a Time Bank that only ever grows, even on your bad days.
• Pick a real goal (learn an instrument, get in shape, finish a project) and break it into steps.
• Turn reclaimed time into focus sessions that move your goal forward — the app links the time you saved directly to the progress you made.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
• No shame, no guilt trips. Your Time Bank never resets on a bad day — every minute you've ever reclaimed stays counted.
• Built-in daily challenges, streaks, and XP levels keep it feeling like progress, not homework.
• "What could you have done instead?" — honest, specific suggestions based on your own goal and habits, not generic tips.
• Time Budgeting splits your day across what matters to you (goals, school, health, social, entertainment) instead of one flat number.
• Personal pattern insights and recovery plans when a goal falls behind — real coaching, computed from your own data.
• Works fully offline — install it to your home screen, and your data stays on your device.

PREMIUM ($1.99/month)
Unlock unlimited goals, time budgeting, "what could I have done instead," personal behavior patterns, goal recovery plans, advanced weekly reports, and historical trends. The free version stays genuinely useful — Premium just goes deeper.

No ads. No tracking. No selling your data — there's nothing to sell, since it never leaves your device except the email you use to unlock Premium.
