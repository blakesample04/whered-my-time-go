About

Hi, I'm Blake Sample.

I paint landscapes in acrylic, mostly on small canvases. Many of them are places I've stood in myself: a stone bridge on a morning walk, a canyon trail, a field just after sunset.

I build each painting out of short, square brushstrokes. Up close they read as patches of color, and from across the room they settle into sky, trees and water. I'm drawn to the moment light changes: pink clouds at dusk, the last sun on a ridge, mist lifting off a pond.

Now and then I paint a study of a work I love, like my takes on Dalí's melting clocks and Magritte's lit house under a daytime sky. They're how I learn.
