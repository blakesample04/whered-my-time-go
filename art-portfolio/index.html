Hi, I'm Blake Sample.

I paint landscapes in acrylic, mostly on small canvases. I'm drawn to scenes with interesting light, color, and atmosphere, whether I'm painting water, a cloudy afternoon, or the last bit of light before sunset.

My paintings are built from lots of short, blocky brushstrokes. Up close they read as patches of color, and from across the room they settle into sky, trees and water. I enjoy playing with color and seeing how much I can capture with just a few shapes and brushstrokes.
